# E-Grass Power Lab

**Live demo:** https://falakanand2008-cpu.github.io/egrass/

An interactive model of the E-Grass System, a biomass power plant fuelled by elephant grass that my team designed for the GIC sustainable energy competition (3rd place).

## How the plant works
Burning elephant grass heats water in a sealed container. When steam pressure builds up, an organic material cap pops (like a popcorn kernel) and releases the steam into a turbine. The turbine spins a magnet to generate electricity, which travels by wire to homes. A condenser turns the steam back into water for reuse, and the exhaust passes through filters.

## Features
- Animated schematic of the full plant cycle
- Adjustable fuel type, fuel amount, moisture and cap pressure
- Calculates daily electricity, homes powered, coal replaced and CO₂ avoided
- Compares life-cycle CO₂ against a coal plant

## Built with
HTML, CSS, JavaScript (no libraries)
